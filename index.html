FAQs

Can product images be changed later?

Yes. Product data is kept separately in products.js, so images can be replaced without redesigning the pages.

Are people included in product images?

No. The catalogue is designed for clean product-only imagery.

Does the multilingual cataloger work?

Yes. The Artisan Hub now has a working catalogue generator with six language options.

Is this an official Government of India service?

No. This is an SIH prototype concept designed around the stated problem statement and government-style accessibility principles.
